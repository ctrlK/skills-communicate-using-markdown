# Technical Specifications: Hospital Exam Glove Procurement

## 1. Product Requirements

### 1.1 Material Specifications
- **Primary Material**: Nitrile (synthetic rubber) - latex-free mandatory
- **Thickness**: 
  - Standard exam: 3.5-5.0 mil (fingertip)
  - High-risk procedures: 5.0-6.0 mil minimum
- **Tensile Strength**: ≥18 MPa (ASTM D412)
- **Elongation at Break**: ≥500% (ASTM D412)
- **Modulus at 300%**: ≤12 MPa

### 1.2 Physical Properties
- **AQL (Acceptable Quality Level)**: 1.5 for pinholes (ASTM D5151)
- **Powder-Free**: All gloves must be powder-free
- **Textured Surface**: Fingertips and palm for enhanced grip
- **Beaded Cuff**: Rolled edge to prevent tearing during donning
- **Ambidextrous**: Universal fit for left/right hand

### 1.3 Size Requirements
| Size | Palm Width (mm) | Length (mm) |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| XS   | 76-83          | 220-230     |
| S    | 83-89          | 230-240     |
| M    | 89-102         | 240-250     |
| L    | 102-114        | 250-260     |
| XL   | 114-127        | 260-270     |

## 2. Regulatory Compliance

### 2.1 FDA Requirements
- **510(k) Clearance**: Class I or II medical device
- **Registration**: FDA establishment registration required
- **Labeling**: Compliant with 21 CFR 801
- **Quality System**: ISO 13485 or FDA QSR compliance

### 2.2 Standards Compliance
- **ASTM D6319**: Standard specification for nitrile examination gloves
- **ASTM D5151**: Standard test method for detection of holes in medical gloves
- **ASTM D6977**: Standard specification for examination gloves
- **ISO 11193**: Single-use medical examination gloves requirements
- **EN 455**: Medical gloves for single use (if applicable)

### 2.3 Chemical Safety (USP <800> Compliance)
- **Chemo-Tested**: Permeation testing against hazardous drugs
- **Breakthrough Time**: >30 minutes for common chemotherapy agents
- **Permeation Rate**: <1.0 μg/cm²/min
- **Testing Protocol**: ASTM F739 methodology

## 3. Performance Specifications

### 3.1 Clinical Performance
- **Puncture Resistance**: Minimum force 9N (ASTM F1342)
- **Tear Resistance**: Minimum 2.0N force to initiate tear
- **Tactile Sensitivity**: Compatible with fine motor tasks
- **Viral Penetration**: Zero penetration per ASTM F1671
- **Biocompatibility**: ISO 10993-5 and 10993-10 compliant

### 3.2 Shelf Life & Storage
- **Shelf Life**: Minimum 5 years from manufacture date
- **Storage Conditions**: 
  - Temperature: 50-86°F (10-30°C)
  - Humidity: <80% relative humidity
  - UV Protection: Required for storage areas
- **Packaging Integrity**: Maintains sterility/cleanliness throughout shelf life

## 4. Packaging Requirements

### 4.1 Primary Packaging
- **Dispensing**: 100 or 200 count boxes with easy-dispense design
- **Box Material**: Recyclable cardboard with tear-resistant design
- **Individual Packaging**: Not required for non-sterile gloves
- **Color Coding**: Consistent color scheme by size across all SKUs

### 4.2 Secondary Packaging
- **Case Pack**: 10 boxes per case standard
- **Case Markings**: Size, lot number, expiration date, UPC/barcode
- **Palletization**: Standard 40"x48" pallet configuration
- **Weight**: Maximum 50 lbs per case for ergonomic handling

## 5. Supply Chain Requirements

### 5.1 Manufacturing Standards
- **Facility Certification**: ISO 13485, FDA registered facility
- **Geographic Diversity**: Multiple manufacturing locations preferred
- **Capacity**: Ability to supply 100% of hospital requirements plus 20% surge
- **Lead Times**: Standard 4-6 weeks, emergency 1-2 weeks

### 5.2 Quality Assurance
- **Lot Testing**: Certificate of Analysis for each lot
- **Recall Process**: 24-hour notification, complete traceability
- **Quality Audits**: Annual third-party audits required
- **Statistical Process Control**: Real-time quality monitoring

### 5.3 Inventory Management
- **Fill Rates**: ≥95% on-time, in-full delivery
- **EDI Capability**: Electronic ordering and invoicing
- **Consignment Options**: Available for high-volume items
- **Emergency Stock**: Vendor-managed safety stock agreements
